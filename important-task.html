Important Task

Follow this list to get ready for Regan & Elaina’s wedding cruise.

  1. Book the cruise. First thing to get done is get booked on the cruise through our travel agent, Julia Wile.
  2. Check your passport. Make sure your passport is current and valid up to 6 months after the cruise ends (after September 25, 2027).
  3. Check on flights. These normally are best when booked 8 to 6 months out.
  4. Book a hotel if you’re coming the day before. Julia is going to work on a hotel. You may want to look for an email from her in the next few weeks.
  5. Download the Royal app if you haven’t already.
  6. Check out the shore excursions. Once your deposit is paid you will be able to look at excursions, internet, and drink packages. Book excursions as soon as you can to get the best ones. Internet and drink packages may go on sale in November.
  7. Make payments along the way. Your trip must be paid in full by June 19th.
  8. Start checking reservations mid-July. Some things book up fast. This is for onboard reservations to dining and shows. You can do this as soon as it opens up.
  9. About 45 days out, check in online. Complete online check-in and get your luggage tags.

Questions? Ask in Cruise Chat or contact the travel agent.
