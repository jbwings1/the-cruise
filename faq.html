FAQ

Quick answers for Regan & Elaina’s Symphony of the Seas wedding cruise.

When is the cruise?

We sail September 19–25, 2027 (6 nights) on Royal Caribbean’s Symphony of the Seas.

Where does it leave from?

The ship departs from and returns to Fort Lauderdale, Florida (Port Everglades).

Where does the ship go?

Perfect Day at CocoCay & Western Caribbean. Planned ports include Costa Maya, Cozumel, and Perfect Day at CocoCay (Bahamas), with sea days in between.

How do I book?

Book through our travel agent, Julia Wile — don’t book this group sailing on your own if you want to be with the wedding party. See travel agent information, call 856-332-0557, or visit comesailaway2027.fun.

Do I need a passport?

Most guests will need a valid passport for this itinerary. Check requirements for your citizenship and travel dates, and confirm with the travel agent when you book.

Who’s coming on the cruise?

See the guest list on Who’s Coming. After you book, add your group there — a host will approve it before it shows for everyone.

Can I ask questions or help plan with the group?

Yes — use Cruise Chat to ask questions or plan together. You’ll only need a display name.

What if my question isn’t listed here?

Ask in Cruise Chat, or contact the travel agent for booking and cabin questions.
