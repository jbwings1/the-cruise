Captain’s log · a song for the wedding cruise

The Pirate Song

Elaina Beltran & Regan Roberts

Original words, sung to the tune of Come Sail Away. Story verses follow the quiet opening. “They look to the sea” follows the bigger middle. The lines at the rail lift into the sing-along, the same way the refrain does.

They met at the University of North Dakota, in a physics class held at a local air force base. Regan missed the first days; Elaina handed him her notes. Jeannette spent those two college years with them. Elaina and Jeannette took jobs with Lockheed Martin Flight Service in Prescott, Arizona, and Elaina was heartbroken to leave Regan — until he could come too. Then they started dating, took their first cruise, and never really stopped. Fort Worth came next: law enforcement for Elaina, flight service for Regan. He proposed on a cruise. On September 20, 2027 they marry aboard Symphony of the Seas, sailing September 19–25 from Fort Lauderdale for Costa Maya, Cozumel, and Perfect Day at CocoCay. Soft pastels. The gift they want is your company.

Verse 1 · The chart

She handed the chart
In a physics class on an air-force base
The lad who was late
She gave him her notes, he caught up the pace
Jeannette kept the watch through those college years
A job out in Prescott, she left him ashore
She cried, oh she cried, but she carried on

Verse 2 · He followed

He followed her west
They found him a berth, then the dating began
A cruise of their own
And cruising’s the joy they still hold, hand in hand
Fort Worth was the harbor they made their own
Law for Elaina, flight service for him
And they tried, oh they tried, and they carried on

They look to the sea

They look to the sea
Engaged on a cruise, and it shines in the waves
From heartbreak to joy
The family and friends and the dreams they had
They’ll marry at sea, so the story goes
No registry gold, your company aboard
And they’ll try best they can to carry on

Verse 3 · The wedding crew

She has at her side
Jordan Matthews stands where the honor belongs
Jim Black at his right
Best man on the deck for the vows on that day
Jeannette, Payton, and Dana, bridesmaids all
Ben, Mike, Ryan, Jeff — groomsmen, one and all
So come sail, oh come sail, come and sail away

Along the rail

A gathering of pirates appeared along the rail
They sang to the couple of hope, and this is what they said

They sail in the fall

Soft pastel colors for the wedding day
September the twentieth, the vows are made
So come on, oh come on, come and sail away
