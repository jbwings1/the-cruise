Important Task

Follow this list to get ready for Regan & Elaina’s wedding cruise.

  1. Book the cruise first through our travel agent, Julia Wile.
  2. Check your passport. Make sure it is current and valid through at least March 25, 2028 (6 months after the cruise ends).
  3. Book flights (best about 8 to 6 months out). Fly into Fort Lauderdale (FLL).
  4. Book a hotel if you’re coming the day before. Julia is looking into a group hotel — watch for an email from her in the next few weeks.
  5. Download the Royal app if you haven’t already.
  6. Check out the shore excursions. Once your deposit is paid, you can look at excursions, internet, and drink packages. Book excursions early to get the best ones. Internet and drink packages may go on sale in November.
  7. Make payments along the way so you’re paid in full by June 19th.
  8. Mid-July: book onboard dining and shows. These reservations open around then. Lock them in early so you don’t miss the ones you want.
  9. About 45 days out, check in online in the Royal app or on Royal’s site. Complete online check-in and get your luggage tags.

Questions? Ask in Cruise Chat or contact the travel agent.
